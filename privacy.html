Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

Rally is a group trip planning app for organizing trips, sharing plans, splitting expenses, chatting with trip members, and sharing trip media. This policy explains what information Rally collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Rally is operated by Nikolai Cook, referred to in this policy as "Rally," "we," "us," or "our."

Contact us at rallygrouptrips@gmail.com.

Information we collect

You give us some information directly when you use Rally:

  • Account information. Your phone number (used for SMS sign-in), optional email, display name, avatar, and authentication details.
  • Trip information. Trip names, destinations, dates, descriptions, time zones, roles, members, invitations, stays, events, reservation details, and notes.
  • Expense information. Expense titles, amounts, currencies, payers, participants, split rules, notes, balances, and settlement status.
  • Messages and reactions. Chat messages, replies, edits, emoji reactions, media comments, and related timestamps.
  • Media. Photos, videos, captions, comments, reactions, and related metadata you choose to upload.
  • Live coordination data. During an active trip: your ETA, "heading there" status, and short-lived location pings if you opt in.
  • Support or feedback. Anything you send us directly.

We also collect technical information automatically:

  • Device and app information. Device type, operating system, app version, language, and diagnostics.
  • Push tokens. Push notification tokens issued by Apple or Google.
  • Log and security information. IP address, authentication events, crash logs, error logs, and fraud or abuse signals.
  • Usage information. Interactions with app features, screens, trips, invitations, uploads, and settings.

Trip chat

Trip chat is visible only to the members of that trip. Rally stores your chat messages on our servers, protected in transit by TLS encryption and at rest by our database provider's encryption.

Chat isn't end-to-end encrypted. Rally can technically access message content stored on our servers, and we don't access it except as needed to operate the service, such as delivering messages and push notifications, maintaining security, or as described elsewhere in this policy.

Location

If you opt in, Rally uses your device location during an active trip to share your ETA and "heading there" status with the other people on that trip. Location is requested with your permission and is only used while you're actively sharing in-app.

Live location pings are kept only for the short period needed to provide the feature (typically a few hours) and are then automatically purged. Turning off location sharing in Rally clears your stored coordinates right away, before the auto-purge window. Rally doesn't build long-term location profiles, sell location data, or use it for advertising. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

How we use information

  • Create and manage your Rally account.
  • Let you create, join, organize, and participate in trips.
  • Show trip plans, members, itineraries, expenses, balances, chats, media, and invitations to the appropriate trip members.
  • Send, accept, and manage trip invitations, including by SMS.
  • Send push notifications about trip activity, including previews of chat messages.
  • Power AI features (itinerary suggestions, the in-app concierge, end-of-trip recaps) by sending the inputs needed for those features to our AI provider.
  • Upload, store, process, and display photos and videos you choose to share.
  • Maintain app security, prevent abuse, debug problems, and fix crashes.
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • Improve Rally's features, reliability, and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

How information is shared

Rally is built for group coordination, so some information you add is visible to other people in the relevant trip. Trip members may see your display name, avatar, role, messages, reactions, expenses, ETAs, uploaded media, comments, and other trip content. Your phone number isn't displayed to other users by default.

We share information with service providers that help us operate Rally:

  • Supabase. Authentication (including SMS delivery via its provider), database, realtime sync, file storage, and backend services.
  • Cloudflare. Photo storage (R2) and video storage, transcoding, and delivery (Stream).
  • Anthropic. Large language model inference for AI features such as itinerary suggestions, the concierge, and trip recaps. Inputs sent to Anthropic include the data needed to perform the requested feature.
  • Mapbox. Maps, geocoding, and place lookups.
  • BigDataCloud. Time zone lookup for trip destinations. We send the coordinates of the destination you entered, not your device location.
  • Sentry. Crash and error reporting. Crash reports are associated with a random account identifier, never your name, phone number, or message content.
  • Expo, Apple, and Google. App builds, distribution, push notification delivery, and platform diagnostics.
  • Analytics, messaging, or infrastructure providers we may use to operate and improve the app.

These providers can use information only to provide services to us, protect their services, or comply with law.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect Rally or our users, to investigate abuse or security issues, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

Payments

Rally helps trip members track expenses and suggest settlement amounts. Rally doesn't process peer-to-peer payments. When you settle up, Rally deep-links you to a third-party payment app such as Venmo, Cash App, or Zelle, and the actual payment is handled entirely by that service under its own terms and privacy policy.

If Rally later offers paid digital features, purchases may be processed by Apple App Store, Google Play, or another payment provider, and those providers may process payment and purchase information under their own policies.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Rally, maintain trip history, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Some trip content may stay available to other trip members unless it's deleted or the trip is archived.

We may retain backups, logs, and security records for a limited period after deletion. Live location pings are retained only for the short period needed to provide live coordination features, and are then purged automatically.

Your choices

You choose what information to add to Rally and which trips or media to participate in. You can request access, correction, or export of your personal information by contacting us at rallygrouptrips@gmail.com.

Deleting your account. You can delete your Rally account from inside the app, under Profile. Deleting your account removes your personal information — your phone number, email, display name, avatar, push tokens, and any stored location data — and de-identifies the trip content other members still depend on, such as messages you sent and expenses you paid for or owe on. That content remains in the trip attributed to a removed user, so other members' chat history and balances stay intact. Some information may remain if needed for security, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or in backups for a limited period.

You can also report objectionable content or block another user from inside the app. See our Terms of Service for how we handle reports.

You can manage device permissions (photos, location, notifications) in your device settings at any time.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including transport encryption (TLS) for data in transit and encryption at rest in our database. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee that information will always be secure.

Children

Rally isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Rally, contact us and we'll take appropriate steps to delete it.

International users

Rally may process and store information in the United States and other countries where we or our service providers operate. These countries may have different data protection laws than your country.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date and may provide additional notice in the app or by other reasonable means.

Contact

Questions or requests can be sent to rallygrouptrips@gmail.com.
