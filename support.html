Common questions

How do I add someone to a trip?

Open the trip, go to Invite, and send the invite through your own messages app. If they sign up with the phone number you invited, they land straight in the trip — there's no link to click or code to type.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to the trips screen and tap Delete account. You'll be asked to type DELETE to confirm. This removes your personal information — phone number, email, display name, avatar, push tokens, and any stored location. Messages you sent and expenses you paid for or owe on stay with the trip in de-identified form, so other members' chat history and balances don't break. It can't be undone.

How do I report content or block someone?

To report a chat message, long-press it and choose Report message. Photos and comments have a Report action in the photo viewer. To block someone, open the trip members list and tap Block this person, or use the chat action menu. Blocking hides their messages, photos, and comments from you immediately, and they're never told.

We review every report within 24 hours. See our Terms of Service for our zero-tolerance policy.

Does Rally handle the money?

No. Rally tracks who paid what and works out who owes whom, but settling up hands you off to Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal. The payment happens entirely in that app, and Rally never holds funds.

How does location sharing work?

It's opt-in and off by default. It runs only during an active trip and only while you have it switched on, so the crew can see ETAs. Turning it off in the app clears your stored coordinates right away, and you can revoke the permission entirely in your device settings.

Is trip chat private?

Chat is visible only to members of that trip, protected in transit by TLS and at rest by our database provider's encryption. It isn't end-to-end encrypted — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what that means.

Report a safety issue

If you've seen something that puts someone at risk, email rallygrouptrips@gmail.com with "SAFETY" in the subject line and we'll prioritize it.
